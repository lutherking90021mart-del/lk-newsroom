Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

How do I advertise with LK Newsroom?

Submit a community announcement free of charge. Our editorial team reviews it, and publication is not guaranteed.

Do readers need to pay for news?

No. All public news and features are free. We do not offer paid access, reader memberships or subscriptions.

Can I support LK Newsroom voluntarily?

Yes, support is optional. Contact our team first for verified voluntary-support options. You never need to pay to read or submit news, and support does not buy editorial coverage.

Can I talk to a real person?

Yes. Open the LK Assistant chat and select “Talk to an administrator on WhatsApp” to hand the conversation to our team.
