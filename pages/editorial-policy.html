Editorial Standards

How LK Newsroom sources, labels and updates its content.

LK Newsroom publishes a mixture of original explainers and attributed summaries of external publisher coverage. We aim to make the origin of a story clear and do not present a publisher’s report as first-hand LK Newsroom reporting.

Types of content

LK Newsroom Explainers: background guides prepared from public primary sources, official data and documents. Each explainer links to the materials used and displays a publication or review date.

Publisher briefs: shorter updates based on material made available by an external news publisher or public source. The publisher is identified on the story. These briefs are not a claim that an LK Newsroom correspondent attended an event or independently witnessed it.

Live scores and publisher videos: third-party data or publisher material, identified as such and subject to the terms and availability of the original provider.

Sources and verification

For explainers, we prioritise primary material such as official statistics, laws, public reports, institutional statements and original datasets. We check the date, scope and definitions used in a source before drawing conclusions. Where a source does not support a point, we do not present that point as established fact.

For external briefs, readers should consult the linked publisher report for the full context. RSS or other automated feeds may assist with discovery and formatting; automation is not evidence that a story has been independently confirmed by an LK Newsroom reporter.

Headlines, images and attribution

Headlines should accurately reflect the available material. Images should be relevant to the subject and should not imply that a file photograph depicts the specific event unless that is confirmed. External publishers and public sources are credited where the story relies on them.

Automation and editorial responsibility

Digital tools may assist with feed collection, transcription, research organisation and presentation. They do not create first-hand reporting. Content presented as an LK Newsroom original explainer must be reviewed for accuracy, source links, date context and clarity before publication. If that review has not taken place, the item must not be described as independently reported or verified.

Corrections and feedback

Readers can request a correction by providing the story URL, the exact passage in question and reliable supporting evidence. The process is explained in our Corrections Policy. Contact martinluther@lknewsroom.online.
