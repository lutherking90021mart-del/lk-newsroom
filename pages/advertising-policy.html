Advertising and Sponsored Content Policy

How LK Newsroom distinguishes advertising from editorial material.

Last updated: 8 November 2026. Advertising helps support the operation of LK Newsroom. Commercial relationships must remain distinguishable from editorial coverage, and advertising must not be presented as independent reporting.

Advertising labels

Google AdSense placements are advertising supplied by Google and its advertising partners. Direct campaigns sold or managed by LK Newsroom should be labelled “Advertisement” or “Sponsored” where appropriate. Paid partnerships, sponsored articles and other paid editorial-style placements must carry a clear disclosure identifying the commercial relationship and sponsor.

Editorial independence

Advertisers do not receive a guarantee of favourable news coverage in exchange for buying advertising. Advertising decisions should not determine the facts, conclusions or wording of an editorial article. The newsroom may decline or remove advertising that is unlawful, deceptive, inappropriate for the audience or inconsistent with the site’s policies.

Reader choice

Readers are not asked to click advertisements to support LK Newsroom. Click an advertisement only if you are genuinely interested in the product or service. Invalid clicks, repeated clicks intended to increase revenue, or any attempt to manipulate ad activity are not welcome.

Where ads may appear

Advertising placements should be limited to pages with sufficient publisher content and should not obscure navigation, imitate editorial controls, or appear on empty, under-construction or low-value screens. LK Newsroom may withhold placements from short external publisher briefs, legal pages, category-only lists and other screens that do not provide substantial additional value.
