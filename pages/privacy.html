Privacy Policy

How LK Newsroom handles information, analytics, browser storage and advertising.

Last updated: 8 November 2026. This policy explains the information that may be processed when you use LK Newsroom at lknewsroom.online.

Information you choose to provide

If you submit a comment, the site receives the display name and comment text you enter. Comments may be held for moderation before publication. If you subscribe to the LK Daily Brief, the site receives the email address you submit. If you contact us by email or WhatsApp, the information in that message is handled by the email or messaging provider you choose and by the newsroom when it receives the message.

Technical and usage information

To operate and protect the site and understand how it is used, the service may process page URLs, article identifiers, referrer or traffic source, device category, browser, operating system, visitor and session identifiers, and interaction events such as page views, article opens, scroll depth and shares. The site’s analytics code creates a random visitor identifier in browser local storage and a session identifier in session storage. These identifiers are not designed to contain your name or email address.

Cookies and browser storage

The site may use browser storage for preferences such as the selected display theme, a temporary copy of recently loaded headlines, analytics identifiers and advertisement-frequency settings. Clearing browser storage may remove these preferences or cached headlines. Third-party embedded content and links, including YouTube and social platforms, may use their own cookies or similar technologies when you interact with them.

Google advertising

LK Newsroom uses Google AdSense advertising code. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure or personalise ads, depending on your location, privacy choices and applicable requirements. Read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and manage ad personalisation through Google Ads Settings.

Where required for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, publishers using Google advertising products must use an appropriate Google-certified consent management platform for personalised ads. Google explains these requirements in its publisher consent-management guidance.

Service providers and external websites

LK Newsroom uses Cloudflare for website hosting, delivery and related services. The site code can use Supabase for connected functions in environments where that service is configured. External publisher links, embedded videos, social networks and Google advertising operate under their own terms and privacy practices. We cannot control information collected by those services after you leave LK Newsroom or interact with their content.

How information is used

  • To display the site and deliver news, explainers and features you request.
  • To moderate comments and respond to news tips, messages and correction requests.
  • To store and manage newsletter subscriptions.
  • To maintain security, diagnose errors and understand aggregate site usage.
  • To display, measure and manage advertising where enabled.

Your choices

You can manage cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Blocking some storage or third-party technologies may affect preferences, embedded media or other site features. You can use Google Ads Settings to manage personalised advertising. You can contact the newsroom to ask about information you have submitted directly to LK Newsroom.

Contact about privacy

For privacy questions, contact martinluther@lknewsroom.online. Please do not send passwords, payment card details or other sensitive information through a general enquiry.
