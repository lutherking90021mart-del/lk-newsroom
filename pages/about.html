About LK Newsroom

Ghana-focused news, source-led explainers and coverage of Africa and the wider world.

LK Newsroom Global is a Ghana-based digital news and media publisher. We help readers follow public affairs, business, technology, education, health, sport and other stories shaping Ghana, Africa and the wider world. Our tagline is Informing Today, Inspiring Tomorrow.

What readers will find here

LK Newsroom Explainers: background guides that define key terms, describe how a public process works and link to the public documents or datasets used. Explainers are intended to add context beyond a headline and should be checked by an editor before publication.

Publisher briefs: shorter updates based on material made available by an external news publisher or public source. The originating publisher is identified on the story. A publisher brief is not a claim that an LK Newsroom correspondent attended an event or independently witnessed it.

Live and visual coverage: sports scores, publisher video feeds, photo features and other third-party material are identified by provider where applicable and subject to the provider’s availability and terms.

Our editorial responsibility

We aim to make the origin of information clear, use descriptive headlines, keep dates visible and correct factual errors when they are confirmed. When a story relies on an external report, readers should be able to identify and open that source. We do not describe automated feed collection as first-hand reporting.

Our Editorial Standards explain how we distinguish original explainers from external publisher briefs. Our Corrections Policy explains how readers can report a factual concern.
