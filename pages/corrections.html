Corrections Policy

How to raise a factual concern about a published item.

LK Newsroom welcomes specific, evidence-based correction requests. We distinguish factual errors from differences of opinion, interpretation or prediction. A correction request is reviewed against the available source material and the wording of the published item.

How to submit a request

  1. Email martinluther@lknewsroom.online.
  2. Include the full article URL and quote the exact sentence, number, name or date you believe is wrong.
  3. Explain what should be corrected and provide a link to an official record, source document or other reliable evidence where possible.
  4. Tell us whether the concern involves accuracy, attribution, privacy or a potentially harmful error.

How corrections are handled

When a factual error is confirmed, the relevant passage should be corrected and a note added to the article identifying that a correction was made and the date of the change. Material changes to a story should not be silently presented as though the original wording had never appeared. If a publisher brief is based on a third-party report, we may also contact or point readers to the original publisher where the underlying report needs correction.

Updates and clarifications

Some developing stories change as authorities release new information. A normal update that adds later information is not necessarily a correction. Where the original item was inaccurate or materially misleading, we will identify the change as a correction or clarification.

Right of reply

Individuals and organisations referred to in our coverage can contact us with a concise response and supporting information. Please include the article URL and a contact method. Publication of a response is considered editorially and is not automatic.
